Pricing Questions

What Affects Your Fee

Because a single W-2 return and a two-state S-Corp with rental income are not the same job. Publishing one number would either overcharge simple filers or undercharge complex ones. After a short consultation we know exactly which forms your return needs — and we quote you a flat fee in writing before starting.

The main drivers are: the number of income sources and forms (W-2, 1099-NEC, 1099-K, K-1), the schedules required (C, E, SE, D), how many states you file in, whether you have business or rental activity, dependents and credits claimed, and how organized your records are when you send them.

You'll agree to the fee in writing before preparation begins. Payment options are discussed during your consultation, and depending on your situation, fees may be able to come out of your refund rather than out of pocket.
