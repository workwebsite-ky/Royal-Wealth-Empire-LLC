The Process

Simple, Secure and Completely Virtual

Step 1 — Book Your Free Consultation

Call, text or send the contact form. We'll discuss your income sources, dependents, business activity and prior filings, then confirm exactly what your return requires and what it will cost.

Step 2 — Upload Your Documents Securely

You'll receive a personalised checklist and a secure upload link. Photograph documents with your phone or attach PDFs — whatever's easiest. Nothing gets emailed unencrypted.

Step 3 — Preparation & Double Review

Your return is prepared, then reviewed a second time against every credit and deduction that applies. We walk you through the numbers before anything is transmitted.

Step 4 — E-Sign, E-File & Track

Sign electronically from your phone. We transmit to the IRS and your state, confirm acceptance, and keep you updated until your refund or balance is settled.
