In Her Own Words

The Tax Industry Changed My Life

I'm an entrepreneur, mentor, and leader who is passionate about helping individuals and families build financial freedom through tax preparation and financial literacy.

Four years ago, I entered the tax industry looking for more than just another stream of income. I was looking for financial freedom, stability, and an opportunity to create a better future for myself and my family.

Like many of you, I know what it feels like to live paycheck to paycheck. I know what it's like to wonder how the bills will get paid, to carry the weight of being a single mother, and to keep showing up even when life feels overwhelming.

The tax industry gave me confidence. It gave me purpose. It gave me knowledge. Most importantly, it showed me that wealth isn't just about money — it's about creating opportunities that can change generations.

The journey hasn't been easy. I've faced setbacks. I've experienced disappointment. I've had to start over more than once. I've been underestimated, overlooked, and misunderstood. But every obstacle became a lesson, and every lesson made me stronger.

Today, I'm proud to say that I'm no longer just preparing tax returns. I'm building leaders.

Brittany “Bamz” Hill — Founder & CEO
